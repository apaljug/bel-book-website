Jaira Belen

author · painter · songwriter · naturalist

Committed to her path, Jaira Belen is a promising author, singer, painter, artist, naturalist, and songwriter — a revolutionary person who recently shared her very first book with the world.

So far the book has gotten nothing but great feedback, from people of all ages and genders, from everywhere in the world, setting their eyes on the uniqueness and captivating style of her art.

Jaira Belen is also pursuing her studies in anthropology — a human being worth pouring your curiosity into, as she unfolds all the abundant and magnificent paths a woman can live, opening herself to the world with no fear and true passion.
