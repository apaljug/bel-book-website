Signature work

Arches, done right.

Arched doors are some of the hardest pieces in finish carpentry: curved jambs, curved casing and a door that has to swing cleanly through it all. They're also the work I'm proudest of.

Whether you're restoring a Spanish-style home or adding character to a new one, I can build arched doors, openings, niches and gates to match.
